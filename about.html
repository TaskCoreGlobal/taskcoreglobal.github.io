About TaskCore Global LTD

Your Trusted Partner for Seamless Corporate Operations.

Our Vision: Simplifying Complexity

At TaskCore Global LTD, we believe that businesses should focus on their core competencies, not get bogged down by the complexities of operational tasks. Born from a vision to revolutionize corporate service procurement, we set out to create a singular, reliable partner for companies of all sizes.

Our journey began with identifying a crucial gap: the fragmented nature of corporate services. Companies often spend valuable time and resources sourcing, vetting, and managing multiple vendors for different tasks, from printing and cleaning to complex installations. We envisioned a solution that would eliminate this inefficiency, providing a comprehensive, quality-assured, and seamless experience.

Today, TaskCore Global LTD stands as that solution. We pride ourselves on our meticulous approach to understanding client needs, our rigorous vetting process for subcontractors, and our unwavering commitment to oversight and quality. We're not just a service provider; we are an extension of your operational team, dedicated to ensuring every task is executed perfectly, under one reliable banner.

Our People

About the Team

The leadership behind TaskCore Group: the people who set our direction, run our operations and keep every project moving.

Founder & Chairman, TaskCore Group

Momoreoluwa Adewale

Momoreoluwa Adewale is the Founder and Chairman of TaskCore Group, a Nigerian business group focused on delivering integrated solutions across contracting, technology, infrastructure and logistics.

A Statistics graduate of Bowen University, Momoreoluwa approaches business through a combination of analytical thinking, commercial strategy and practical execution. He founded TaskCore with the belief that clients should not have to coordinate multiple disconnected providers to solve interconnected operational problems.

Under his leadership, TaskCore has developed experience delivering projects for government institutions and organisations across ICT infrastructure, facility modernization, connectivity, LAN and internet systems, CCTV and security infrastructure, intercom systems, multimedia and conference solutions, office transformation, technical installations and ongoing support.

Beyond individual projects, his focus is on building the systems, partnerships and operating structure that allow TaskCore to take on increasingly complex assignments. This has driven the Group’s expansion beyond technology contracting into complementary sectors, including logistics and other infrastructure-driven opportunities.

As Chairman, Momoreoluwa sets the Group’s strategic direction and plays a central role in business development, strategic partnerships, major client relationships and new ventures.

His long-term vision is to build TaskCore into a trusted, multi-sector Nigerian business group known for one thing above all: the ability to take a complex problem and deliver a practical, coordinated solution.
